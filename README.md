# Federico Nutarelli

<img align="left" width="200" src="federico.jpg" />


I am an Assistant Professor in Economics at <a href="https://axes.imtlucca.it/">IMT Lucca</a> and a long-term visitor at <a href="https://www.tse-fr.eu/people/federico-nutarelli">Toulouse School of Economics</a>.

I am mainly interested in empirical industrial organization, with a focus on pharmaceutical markets, innovation, and firms’ responses to shocks.

My work combines microdata with causal inference and machine-learning methods to study pricing, innovation incentives, and firm dynamics in healthcare and international trade.
